Section I
Strategic Context

The Problem Every Campaign Faces

Social media influencers now determine election outcomes. Campaigns that reach the right voices — quickly — gain an insurmountable ground-level advantage. The current process for identifying those voices is broken.

Current State — The Failure
✗
Campaign staff spend 40+ hours per cycle manually searching platforms, reviewing profiles, and building prospect lists — time that should be spent on voter outreach.
✗
Manual searches are inconsistent and subjective. Different staff members apply different criteria, producing unreliable results that vary by person and mood.
✗
General influencer marketing tools are built for consumer brands, not political campaigns. They lack political topic filters, geographic targeting, and civic relevance scoring.
✗
By the time research is compiled, the political moment has passed. Campaigns are reactive, not proactive, losing the narrative advantage to faster opponents.
AL-MOG.IO — The Solution
✓
A campaign manager sets parameters once. The platform returns a fully ranked shortlist in under 30 seconds — across all five major social media platforms simultaneously.
✓
Every result is scored 0–100 by an AI agent trained on political relevance signals: keyword alignment, geographic reach, engagement authenticity, and topic credibility.
✓
Built exclusively for political use. Filters are purpose-designed: political topic taxonomy, election-cycle targeting, constituency-level geography, and language.
✓
The AI engine — Llama 3.3 70B via Groq — is open-source and free at scale. No proprietary vendor lock-in. No per-query licensing costs.
Section II
Platform Capabilities

What the Platform Delivers

Six core capabilities, each engineered for political campaign conditions where speed, precision, and credibility are non-negotiable.

01
Multi-Platform Discovery
Simultaneous search across Instagram, X (Twitter), YouTube, TikTok, and Facebook. A single query reaches every major platform without platform-switching or duplicated effort.
02
AI Relevance Scoring
Every influencer profile receives a 0–100 score generated by Llama 3.3 70B. The model weighs keyword alignment, political topic match, engagement authenticity, and audience geography.
03
Political Topic Taxonomy
A curated taxonomy of 20 political issue areas — healthcare, economy, immigration, climate, education, criminal justice, and more — enables precision targeting beyond generic keyword search.
04
Campaign-Grade Filtering
Configurable parameters for every search: constituency-level location, follower count range, minimum engagement threshold, language, and platform selection — all preserved per campaign.
05
AI-Generated Intelligence
Each ranked result includes a written AI brief explaining precisely why this individual is relevant to this campaign — actionable intelligence that can be presented directly to senior staff or donors.
06
Tiered Access Control
A free tier for initial qualification. A PRO tier for full operational use — 100 results per search, CSV and PDF export, unlimited daily searches, and real social API data feeds.
Section III
Operational Use Cases

Who Uses This Platform and How

Four validated operational scenarios across the political campaign ecosystem.

Use Case A
The Senate Candidate
Statewide race. 18-month campaign window. Healthcare and economic recovery as primary platforms. Target constituency: Ohio, suburban and rural districts.
"I need to identify which local content creators already speak to my voters — people my constituency trusts — before my opponent does. I cannot afford to contact the wrong voices or spend weeks building a prospect list manually."
Operational Flow
  • 1. Sets keywords: "healthcare reform," "Ohio economy," "small business"
  • 2. Configures location filter: Ohio, minimum 25,000 followers
  • 3. Selects platforms: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook
  • 4. AI agent returns 10 ranked profiles with individual relevance scores and written briefs
  • 5. Campaign communications director contacts top 3 within 24 hours
Outcome: 40+ hours of manual research replaced by a 30-second query. Campaign staff redirected to voter mobilization.
Use Case B
The Campaign Manager
Managing three simultaneous state legislative campaigns — Texas, Florida, and Georgia — with a shared central team and limited research capacity.
"My team is running three races at once. I need a systematic way to build influencer shortlists for each campaign, compare coverage across states, and ensure we are not approaching the same creator with conflicting campaign messages."
Operational Flow
  • 1. Executes separate searches per state, each with state-specific keywords and topics
  • 2. Exports CSV reports for each campaign (PRO tier)
  • 3. Identifies influencer overlap across campaign territories
  • 4. Distributes shortlists to each campaign's communications director
  • 5. Re-runs searches monthly to capture emerging voices
Outcome: Centralized intelligence operation supporting three independent campaigns from a single platform instance.
Use Case C
The PAC Director
National issue-advocacy organization. Climate and energy policy focus. Operating across 12 target states ahead of midterm elections.
"We represent a specific policy position. We need content creators who have an established, credible history of discussing our issues — not opportunists who will accept any paid placement. The AI brief tells us who is genuine."
Operational Flow
  • 1. Keywords: "climate change," "green energy," "carbon policy," "clean energy jobs"
  • 2. No location filter — national scope; minimum 200,000 followers, 5% engagement floor
  • 3. Selects all five platforms; AI scores content-topic alignment against policy brief
  • 4. Reviews AI intelligence briefs to verify content credibility and audience alignment
  • 5. Builds a national outreach list of 50 qualified creators across all target states
Outcome: A pool of 50,000 candidate creators is filtered to 50 qualified, policy-aligned targets in under three minutes.
Use Case D
The Political PR Firm
Agency representing 12 political clients across six states. Provides influencer strategy as a billable service. Requires data-backed deliverables for client presentations.
"Our clients retain us for intelligence, not opinion. When we present an influencer recommendation, it must be defensible with data and AI-generated reasoning. We cannot walk into a campaign meeting and say we found someone by searching Instagram manually."
Operational Flow
  • 1. Creates a dedicated search profile per client, preserving their specific parameters
  • 2. Generates PDF and CSV reports with AI scores and written intelligence briefs
  • 3. Presents ranked shortlists as primary deliverable in client strategy sessions
  • 4. Monitors for influencer conflicts across client portfolios
  • 5. Re-runs analysis quarterly and before major campaign milestones
Outcome: AI-backed influencer intelligence becomes a premium, repeatable, high-margin service offering for the firm.

Section VII
Technical Foundation

Architecture & Technology Stack

Built on modern open-source infrastructure. No proprietary AI licensing. No single-vendor dependency. Deployable to any major cloud provider.

Framework
Next.js 14 — App Router
Full-stack TypeScript application. Server-side rendering, static generation, and API routes in a single deployable unit. Production-ready from day one.
AI Engine
Groq + Llama 3.3 70B
Open-source large language model hosted on Groq infrastructure. 7,500 free requests per day. JSON mode for deterministic structured output. No Anthropic or OpenAI dependency.
Data — Free
Seeded Model Data
Realistic influencer profiles generated from search parameters using a deterministic seeding algorithm. Indistinguishable from live data in structure and format. No API keys required.
Data — PRO
YouTube API v3 · Twitter/X API v2
Live platform data via official APIs. Integration stubs for Instagram Graph API, TikTok Research API, and Facebook Graph API ready for activation in Milestone 1 and 2.
Database
Prisma ORM — SQLite / PostgreSQL
SQLite for development and POC. PostgreSQL for production from Milestone 1 onward. Schema covers search history, user sessions, campaign workspaces, and outreach tracking.
Hosting
Vercel · Railway · Any Cloud
The application is cloud-agnostic. Vercel provides zero-config Next.js deployment with global edge network. Railway or AWS for database hosting. Deployable on-premises if required.
